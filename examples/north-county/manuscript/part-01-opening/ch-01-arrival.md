## Bus Stop
<!-- openscribe-scene-id: scene-33ae8ab69adc4c66835475a4e5e7dec6 -->

Eli stepped off the bus into wet summer heat and the smell of cut hay.

The station was only a pad of cracked concrete beside a grocery with dim windows and a hand painted sign.
North County looked smaller than the map had promised.

## Porch Watchers
<!-- openscribe-scene-id: scene-db6421e644fc4443a2e239271d84348a -->

Across the road, three men on a porch stopped talking when they saw him.
One lifted two fingers from a coffee cup.
The others just stared.

## Willow Lane
<!-- openscribe-scene-id: scene-7af1a16e7675442abba2eee8561c08a9 -->

Eli checked the paper in his pocket again even though he knew the address by heart.
Gray house.
Tin roof.
End of Willow Lane.

He started walking before he could decide to turn back.
